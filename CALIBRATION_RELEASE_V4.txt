Calibration release v4

- A held move away from an already-triggered, known endstop no longer stops at OPEN.
  It continues in the same hold toward the opposite physical endstop.
- Automatic endstop stop retires the browser-side hold immediately, so the opposite
  direction is available as soon as the carriage is parked on the switch.
- Same-direction motion into the active endstop remains blocked.
- No software safe-range was added back.
