FlowEngine Personal — calibration endstop escape v5

Fixes a false fatal fault when leaving a latched X/Y endstop during commissioning.

- Physical TRIGGERED + last approach direction is now the authority for which direction is blocked.
- M114 proximity is no longer used as an interlock on unreferenced X/Y axes.
- Motion in the same direction that entered the active endstop is blocked non-fatally.
- The opposite direction remains available immediately and releases the switch before seeking the other edge.
- A blocked-direction attempt no longer calls abort(), no longer invalidates the session, and no longer requires reconnect.
- If an endstop is active without approach context, the session remains connected and reports side-unknown instead of faulting the transport.

Tests added for real-hardware-like M114 drift while an endstop remains pressed and for wrong-direction-then-reverse recovery.
